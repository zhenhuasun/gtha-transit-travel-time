Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area by transit

How long does it really take to cross Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area by train? Set a start point: the whole city is colored by travel time, computed from the GO Transit network's official schedules.

Launch the Map

How it works

  1. 1. Pick a start An address, a station, or your location: the map colors around it.
  2. 2. Click a destination You get the duration and the itinerary: which line to take, transfers, walking.
  3. 3. Compare The 15- and 30-minute curves show what is really close, by subway, tram, or bus.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the travel times come from?

From the official scheduled timetables of the GO Transit network, published as open data in GTFS format. They cover an ordinary weekday, between 7 a.m. and 8 p.m.

Are the displayed times reliable?

These are "on paper" averages: walk to the stop, waiting time equal to half the interval between two services, scheduled time between stops, and transfers. No real-time data or disruptions.

Are buses included?

Yes, optionally on the map. By default, only subways and trams are shown, to highlight the backbone of the network.

Who made this site?

The idea comes from NYC Transit Time Cartogram by Anthony Castrio, later adapted to Paris by Jules Grandin (C'est encore loin ?), then extended by Camille Roux — the concept and engineering are his work. This Toronto edition is a fork by zhenhuasun, using TTC schedules. The code is open on GitHub.
