Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area by Transit

How long does it really take to cross Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area by transit? Choose a starting location and explore travel times across GTHA by train, subway, streetcar, and bus using official schedule data.

Launch the Map

A trip that looks short on a map may take an hour by transit. This tool helps visualize GTHA through actual travel times by transit, making it easier to compare neighbourhood accessibility, commute options, and transit connectivity.

How it works

  1. 1. Choose a starting point Enter an address, select a station, or use your current location.
  2. 2. Explore the network Click anywhere on the map to view travel times, routes, transfers, and walking connections.
  3. 3. Compare accessibility See which GTHA neighbourhoods are reachable within 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes by transit.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the travel times come from?

Travel times are calculated using official GO Transit and municipal transit schedules published in GTFS (General Transit Feed Specification) format. The map represents a typical weekday service pattern between 7:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.

How accurate are the travel times?

The results are schedule-based estimates. They include walking time, average waiting time, scheduled in-vehicle travel time, and transfers. Real-time delays, congestion, service disruptions, and crowding are not included.

Are buses included?

Yes. Train, subway, streetcar, and bus services can all be included in travel-time calculations.
