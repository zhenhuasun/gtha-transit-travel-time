Toronto by Transit

Explore Toronto through transit travel time, not distance. Choose a starting location and explore travel times across Toronto by subway, streetcar, and bus using official schedule data.

Launch the Map

A trip that looks short on a map may take an hour by transit. This tool helps visualize Toronto through actual travel times by transit, making it easier to compare neighbourhood accessibility, commute options, and transit connectivity.

How it works

  1. 1. Choose a starting point Enter an address, select a station, or use your current location.
  2. 2. Explore the network Click anywhere on the map to view travel times, routes, transfers, and walking connections.
  3. 3. Compare accessibility See which Toronto neighbourhoods are reachable within 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes by transit.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the travel times come from?

Travel times are calculated using official TTC schedules published in GTFS (General Transit Feed Specification) format. The map represents a typical weekday service pattern between 7:00 a.m. and 8:00 p.m.

How accurate are the travel times?

The results are schedule-based estimates. They include walking time, average waiting time, scheduled in-vehicle travel time, and transfers. Real-time delays, congestion, service disruptions, and crowding are not included.

Are buses included?

Yes. Subway, streetcar, and bus services can all be included in travel-time calculations. Some map views may emphasize rapid transit to highlight the core TTC network.
