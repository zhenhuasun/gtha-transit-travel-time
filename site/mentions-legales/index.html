About this site

The GTHA by Transit is done by Joshua Sun. The idea of the work is from montpellier-temps-transport by Camille Roux. The original idea comes from Anthony Castrio's NYC Transit Time Cartogram, later adapted to Paris by Jules Grandin, then extended by Camille Roux.

Hosting

GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages), 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States.

Personal data

No audience measurement, no cookies, no personal identifiers. Trips are computed in your browser: no location or address is stored. Address search queries komoot's Photon API (photon.komoot.io, OpenStreetMap data).
